The method

The Readable Loop.

Boundaries

Questions

Before you ask.

What do I get from the Sprint, exactly?

Four documented deliverables in ten business days: a map of where time and money leak, a diagnosis of the bottlenecks against evidence, and a clear recommended next step — which is sometimes "don't build anything yet."

Do you work with my existing team and tools?

Yes. We design around your reality — your people, your software, your constraints. Ripping everything out is the last resort, not the first move.

Is this surveillance?

No. We measure work, not people. If a system would make your crew feel watched, we won't build it — that's a standing rule, not a preference.

What happens after the Sprint?

You get the deliverables and the recommendation. If ongoing work makes sense, Fractional or Observatory are month-to-month. If not, you keep everything we documented.
