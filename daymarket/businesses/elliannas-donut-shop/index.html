How holidays work here

Four ways Ellianna's shows up.

Morning drops

Deal events announced the night before in the WhatsApp group — members see them an hour before everyone else, then claim in their account.

Waste-turn giveaways

When inventory would go to waste at closing time, it becomes a limited giveaway instead. There's always a catch: you perform an action to earn it — first to answer, first to show up, first to bring a friend.

Scavenger hunts

From time to time the group chat hosts a hunt: clues, a thread, a prize at Ellianna's counter. Attention for the shop, fun for the crew.

The deal platform

When Ellianna's needs to move inventory — a slow week, a big batch, a new item to push — a holiday goes up and the members come. That's the whole point.
