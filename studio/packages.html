Questions

Straight answers.

What counts as one spot?
One finished 30-second video, delivered through your portal. Revisions within the same brief do not count as extra spots.
What happens if I do not use all my spots?
Unused spots do not roll over. Submit early in the month and keep your board moving.
Can I pause?
Yes. Pause anytime and your billing stops. Your board and brand assets stay put for when you come back.
Who owns the videos?
You do. Every finished video is made for you and is 100 percent yours, with full commercial rights.
How fast is turnaround?
Most spots deliver within a few business days of a complete brief. Embedded clients get first-in-line production.
