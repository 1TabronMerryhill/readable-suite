The core idea

Governed learning, human review.

Most AI training teaches features and prompts. The Academy teaches judgment: when AI is appropriate, how to design a workflow around it, and how to verify what it produces.

Every module is human-reviewed. Completion means demonstrating the work against a six-criteria standard — not watching videos. Graduates earn a human-reviewed Certificate of Completion.

The program

Four modules. One standard.

Module 1

Foundations + Safety

What AI systems are, what they can and cannot do, and where the risks live — data exposure, confident errors, and consequential decisions. Safety before speed.

Module 2

Prompting + Verification

Directing AI systems well — and then checking the work. Every output gets evaluated against evidence: accept, revise, reject, or escalate.

Module 3

Practical Application

Real workflows from your actual work: recurring writing, research, coordination, reporting. Built, tested, and documented with human review at every step.

Module 4

Capstone + Human Review

The capstone: evaluate whether AI fits a bounded problem, design the documented workflow, verify the outputs, revise on feedback, and defend the result to a human reviewer.
