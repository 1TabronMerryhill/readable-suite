Four Prices

Every name we trade or are thinking about trading carries four prices (L-013). The disaster price is the business's own floor, built from trough numbers and the balance sheet; it is not a stop and not a walk-away line. When a name we like trades into its disaster zone, we load the boat. Above that sit the early entry and the swing trade price. A price that has not been built yet reads pending, and a package missing any of the four does not trade.

Card values are priced off the lasts above, so they can sit a cent or two off the book. Book totals in the glance come from the same feed as the cards when live, and from the snapshot when it is not.
