Field Network · First Territory

Columbia is Readable's first operational knowledge territory.

We are partnering with a small number of Columbia organizations to document high-load workflows, preserve institutional knowledge, identify hidden operational burdens, and establish AI readiness before expanding the model regionally.

Apply for the Columbia Pilot Meet Readable

Who the pilot is for

Columbia organizations carrying real operational load.

Nonprofits, public agencies, schools, clinics, and local employers — any Columbia organization where critical knowledge lives in people's heads and the team feels the weight of it.

High-load workflows

Work that repeats, matters, and currently depends on one or two people remembering how it goes.

Knowledge at risk

Institutional memory that walks out the door when someone retires, leaves, or burns out.

AI curiosity, honestly held

Leaders who want to know where AI could actually help — and where it should stay out.

Bounded by design

One workflow. Fully documented.

Each pilot organization brings one consequential workflow. Not a department, not a transformation — one piece of work that matters, captured end to end.

  1. Name the workflow. The recurring work with the highest load or the most fragile knowledge.
  2. Authorize the capture. Written permission, agreed boundaries, named participants.
  3. Document it. Structured interviews, observed practice, reviewed documents — the work as it actually happens.
  4. Review it together. Your people verify what we captured before it becomes anything else.

What participants receive

Your workflow, finally written down.

The pilot produces usable artifacts your organization keeps — whether or not anything further happens.

Documented workflow

The full workflow as it actually runs — steps, judgment calls, exceptions, handoffs.

Burden map

Where the hidden operational load sits: the workarounds, the bottlenecks, the single points of failure.

AI opportunity brief

Where AI could responsibly help this workflow — and where humans must stay in charge.

What Readable needs

Access, honesty, and a few hours.

Access

Permission to talk with the people who do the work and observe the workflow in practice.

Honesty

The real process, not the manual version. Workarounds included — that is where the knowledge lives.

A few hours

Interviews and review sessions scheduled around your team's availability, not ours.

Built here, for here

Local flexibility.

Readable is based in Columbia. The pilot team is local, scheduling is local, and the knowledge stays with the organizations that produced it.

No travel budgets, no fly-in consultants, no generic playbooks. The pilot is designed around how Columbia organizations actually operate — small teams, real constraints, community accountability.

Small on purpose

Pilot capacity: three organizations.

Three pilots, done properly, teach more than thirty done thinly. Each organization gets the full lifecycle — authorization through human-reviewed asset — before the model expands regionally.

Admission is competitive and remains subject to scoping, evidence, permission, capacity, and human approval. Applying starts a review, not a partnership.

How we work

Governance, stated plainly.

Readable does not roam, scrape, record, or enter sites without authorization. Every pilot assignment is invited work under written permission.

Your organization's information is used only for your pilot, under the data practices we agree together. Nothing is used to train AI systems. Nothing leaves the agreed boundary. Pilot participants are collaborators in documenting their own work — not research targets.
