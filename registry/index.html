The process

Five steps to being found.

  1. Trust Audit. A human-reviewed evaluation of your business across five dimensions: real, genuine, readable, accurate, legitimate.
  2. Structure. Your operational content is organized into machine-readable schema (JSON-LD) — the language AI search actually reads.
  3. Host. Your AI-legible presence lives on Readable's governed domain. Access is granted by invitation — never passwords.
  4. Browse. Your business appears in the Registry, where buyers and AI systems alike can find it.
  5. Grow. Keep your content fresh and verified; your listing compounds as AI search leans on trusted sources.
